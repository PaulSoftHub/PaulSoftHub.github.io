SKILLS & CAPABILITIES

Skills Built Around Systems, Technology & Improvement

A practical combination of AI, digital systems, automation, executive workflows, and operational excellence focused on solving real-world problems.

01 — AI & PROMPT ENGINEERING

Designing Reusable AI Workflows

Prompt Engineering

Designing structured instructions that guide AI systems toward consistent, useful, and repeatable outputs.

Prompt Systems

Organizing reusable prompts as identifiable, versioned, documented assets rather than one-time chat instructions.

Claude Workflows

Designing practical Claude workflows for recurring professional tasks, research, executive support, review, and analysis.

AI Delegation

Breaking complex work into clear AI-delegable tasks while maintaining appropriate human oversight and approval.

02 — DIGITAL SYSTEMS

Organizing Work Through Digital Infrastructure

GitHub

Using GitHub to organize, version, maintain, and publish structured digital work and documentation.

Markdown

Using Markdown to create lightweight, structured, readable, and maintainable documentation and prompt assets.

Version Control

Applying versioning and controlled changes to digital assets so improvements can be tracked and maintained systematically.

Documentation Systems

Turning knowledge, procedures, standards, workflows, and decisions into structured documentation.

03 — AI AUTOMATION

Connecting Repetitive Workflows with Automation

Workflow Automation

Identifying recurring manual activities that can be structured, simplified, standardized, or partially automated.

AI-Assisted Processes

Integrating AI into professional workflows where it can improve analysis, drafting, classification, summarization, or decision support.

System Integration

Connecting information, tools, workflows, and AI capabilities into practical end-to-end processes.

Human-in-the-Loop

Maintaining appropriate human review, approval, and accountability where automated actions could create business or operational risk.

04 — EXECUTIVE SYSTEMS

Turning Information into Action

Executive Information Management

Structuring important information so that executives can review priorities, risks, deadlines, and decisions efficiently.

Executive Workflows

Designing repeatable workflows around email, meetings, tasks, follow-ups, knowledge, and management review.

Management Review

Organizing information around key indicators, exceptions, risks, actions, and decisions rather than simply presenting raw data.

Decision Support

Structuring information to help management identify what changed, why it matters, and what requires attention.

05 — OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE

Continuous Improvement & Zero-Loss Thinking

TPM

Applying Total Productive Maintenance thinking to performance, maintenance, quality, safety, people, and continuous improvement.

Continuous Improvement

Identifying losses, problems, bottlenecks, and improvement opportunities and converting them into structured improvement activities.

Performance Management

Using structured indicators and review processes to improve visibility, accountability, and management action.

Zero-Loss Thinking

Focusing systematically on eliminating losses, defects, breakdowns, accidents, and other sources of performance deterioration.

WORKING PRINCIPLES

How These Skills Are Applied

01

Business Before Technology

Start with the business or management problem, then select the appropriate technology and workflow.

02

Structure Before Automation

A poorly structured process should not simply be automated. First establish a clear and repeatable workflow.

03

Evidence Before Assumption

Decisions and recommendations should be based on reliable information and clearly identified evidence.

04

Continuous Improvement

Systems should be tested, measured, reviewed, standardized where appropriate, and continuously improved.
